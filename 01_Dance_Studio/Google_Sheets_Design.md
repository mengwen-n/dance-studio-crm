# Dance Studio CRM — Google Sheets Design

## Workbook tabs

建议建立一个 workbook：`DanceFlow_Studio_Operations`。每个 tab 都只保留一行 header，避免 merged cells，方便 Apps Script 和 Looker Studio 读取。

### 1. Leads

```text
Lead_ID | Created_At | Name | Phone | Email | Source | Interest | Trial_Date | Status | Owner | Follow_Up_Date | Last_Contacted_At | Notes
```

Allowed `Status` values:

```text
New lead | Trial booked | Trial attended | Follow-up due | Converted | Lost
```

### 2. Members

```text
Member_ID | Joined_At | Name | Phone | Email | Plan | Class | Start_Date | Renewal_Date | Payment_Status | Attendance_Rate | Status | Notes
```

Allowed `Payment_Status` values:

```text
Paid | Due soon | Overdue | Waived
```

### 3. Classes

```text
Class_ID | Class_Date | Start_Time | Class_Name | Studio_Room | Instructor | Capacity | Booked | Status
```

### 4. Attendance

```text
Attendance_ID | Class_ID | Class_Date | Member_ID | Member_Name | Attendance_Status | Check_In_Time | Recorded_By | Notes
```

Allowed `Attendance_Status` values:

```text
Present | Late | Absent | Excused
```

### 5. Payments

```text
Payment_ID | Payment_Date | Member_ID | Member_Name | Amount | Plan | Payment_Method | Payment_Status | Receipt_Reference | Notes
```

### 6. Automation_Log

```text
Run_ID | Run_At | Automation_Name | Trigger | Record_ID | Action | Result | Error_Message
```

### 7. Crews

```text
Crew_ID | Joined_At | Name | Phone | Email | Crew_Name | Crew_Role | Crew_Status | Annual_Registration_Date | Annual_Fee | Next_Renewal_Date | Notes
```

### 8. Studio_Fees

One row per fee obligation, so the system can track every month separately.

```text
Fee_ID | Crew_ID | Member_Name | Fee_Type | Fee_Month | Due_Date | Amount | Payment_Status | Paid_Date | Receipt_Reference | Approved_By | Notes
```

For the current rule, monthly studio fee rows should use a due date on or before the 20th of each month. The exact date and overdue consequence must be confirmed before automation is activated.

### 9. Settings

```text
Key | Value | Description
Studio_Name | Studio Sol | Display name
Timezone | Asia/Kuala_Lumpur | Automation timezone
Renewal_Alert_Days | 7 | Days before Renewal_Date
Trial_Followup_Hours | 2 | Hours after Trial_Date / class end
Crew_Fee_Due_Day | 20 | Monthly studio fee deadline, pending owner confirmation
Crew_Annual_Registration_Days | 365 | Replace if the studio uses calendar-year renewal
Owner_Email | owner@example.com | Daily summary recipient
```

## Useful formulas

### Follow-ups due today

```gs
=FILTER(Leads!A:M, Leads!K:K<=TODAY(), Leads!I:I<>"Converted", Leads!I:I<>"Lost")
```

### Renewals in the next 7 days

```gs
=FILTER(Members!A:M, Members!I:I>=TODAY(), Members!I:I<=TODAY()+7, Members!L:L="Active")
```

### Crew fees due this month

```gs
=FILTER(Studio_Fees!A:K, Studio_Fees!F:F<=DATE(YEAR(TODAY()),MONTH(TODAY()),20), Studio_Fees!H:H<>"Paid")
```

### Trial-to-member conversion rate

```gs
=COUNTIF(Leads!I:I,"Converted")/COUNTIF(Leads!I:I,"Trial attended")
```

### Attendance rate

```gs
=COUNTIF(Attendance!F:F,"Present")/COUNTA(Attendance!A:A)
```

## Data rules

- IDs are generated by Apps Script, not manually typed.
- Dates are stored as real date/time values, not display strings.
- Never delete historical rows; use a status such as `Lost`, `Cancelled`, or `Inactive`.
- Phone numbers should be stored as text so leading `+60` is not lost.
- Public demos must use fictional data only.
- `Studio_Fees` must keep one row per month; do not overwrite last month's payment with this month's status.
